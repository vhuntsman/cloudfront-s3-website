Designing a calmer home

September 18, 2026 · 6 min read

A calmer home does not require a minimalist makeover. Often, small changes to lighting, storage and visual clutter have the biggest effect.

Start with the entryway

Create a predictable place for keys, bags and shoes. The goal is not perfection; it is reducing the number of decisions made when you arrive home.

Use fewer competing materials

Repeated materials create visual continuity. Choose two or three finishes and let them recur throughout the room.

Make storage easy

The best storage system is the one people actually use. Open baskets and accessible shelves often outperform complicated solutions.
