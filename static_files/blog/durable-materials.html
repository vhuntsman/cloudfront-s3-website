Choosing durable materials

September 4, 2026 · 8 min read

When choosing materials, consider maintenance, repairability and how the surface will look after years of use—not just how it looks on day one.

Timber

Timber is warm and repairable, but different species and finishes behave very differently in high-traffic areas.

Stone

Natural stone can be extremely durable, although porosity and sealing requirements vary considerably.
